Research

Twenty institutes, one research culture.

Beyond the 53 teaching departments, the university runs twenty dedicated research institutes and centres — from world-recognized chemistry to marine biology on the Arabian Sea.

H.E.J. Research Institute of Chemistry

The university’s prime research institute, designated by the World Academy of Sciences and internationally recognised for natural product chemistry. Part of the International Center for Chemical & Biological Sciences (ICCBS).
