Introduction

One of Karachi’s first computer science departments.

The Department of Computer Science was established by a resolution of the Academic Council on 27 November 1984, and began functioning in the 1985–86 academic year — offering a Master of Computer Science (MCS) degree and becoming one of the first institutions in Karachi to teach Computer Science and Technology.

The department maintains close links with professional organizations at national and international levels, and revises its course content every two years to keep pace with developments in the field — curricula are built in line with the recommendations of the Sindh Task Force on Computer Science Programs.

Department History

Four decades of teaching computer science.

1984

Department established

Founded by resolution of the Academic Council on 27 November 1984.

1985–86

MCS degree program begins

The department starts functioning with a Master of Computer Science degree — among the first in Karachi.

1995

Evening MCS & PGD launched

A self-finance evening MCS program starts, alongside a Post Graduate Diploma in Computer & Information Sciences, to meet growing demand for skilled professionals.

1996

BS (Computer Science) introduced

A four-year morning BS degree launches, leading into a two-year MS and, beyond that, a PhD program.

1999

First BS batch graduates

The first cohort of the morning BS (Computer Science) program completes its degree.

2001

Evening BS program added

The four-year BS degree is extended to the evening session.
