Our History

Established by an act of parliament, seventy-five years ago.

Today the University of Karachi is the largest university in Pakistan: eight faculties, fifty-three departments, and twenty research institutes and centres, serving more than 41,000 students under the guidance of over 700 faculty members.

1950

The Karachi University Act is passed on 23 October, enacted the following year with Prof. A. B. A. Haleem as the university’s first Vice Chancellor.

1953

Teaching begins for the first time — two faculties, fifty students — after two years spent solely examining the affiliated colleges of Sindh.

1960

The campus moves to its 1,279-acre site on University Road, on 18 January — a date still marked jointly by alumni and the administration each year.

2026

The Platinum Jubilee: seventy-five years of teaching, research, and public service from the same address.

Research

Where Pakistani chemistry earned its global reputation.

The H.E.J. Research Institute of Chemistry is designated by the World Academy of Sciences — the country’s foremost centre for natural product chemistry, and one of twenty institutes housed across the campus.
