Introduction

Housed in a building that resembles a ship.

The Central Library of the University of Karachi occupies a unique building at the heart of the campus — its architecture famously resembles a huge ship. It is the hub of academic and research activity for one of the country’s largest universities.

Founded in 1952, the library was renamed Dr. Mahmud Husain Library in 1975, following the death of the former Vice Chancellor and renowned scholar, in recognition of his services to scholarship, education, and the promotion of librarianship in Pakistan.

Its holdings include more than 350,000 books and an equal number of bound research and scientific journal volumes. The library also carries the status of a United Nations depository centre.

The Quaid-e-Azam Collection
The personal book collection of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah, stored in its original antique cabinets — alongside photographs, letters, and posters from the Freedom Movement.

Part of a rare-books collection of roughly 6,000 volumes dating to the 16th century, and the personal libraries of 46 scholars and academicians.
