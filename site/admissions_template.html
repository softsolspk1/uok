How It Works

From prospectus to enrolment.

01

Read the prospectus

Each pathway publishes its own prospectus and advertisement covering eligibility, seats, and quotas before the form opens.

02

Submit your application & entrance test form

Complete the online form for your programme and generate your entrance test processing-fee voucher.

03

Pay the processing fee

Submit payment by the listed deadline — extensions, when granted, are posted against each pathway.

04

Sit the entrance test or interview

Test centre and admit-card details are published per programme ahead of the test date.

05

Check the provisional merit list

Results and closing percentages are posted by department; valid claims are listed separately.

06

Confirm your seat

Complete fee submission to confirm enrolment and receive your student ID.
