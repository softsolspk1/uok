Chairperson: Dr. Zeenat Haroon (Assoc. Prof.)
The Department was established in 2000. Master’s Degree Program is available in this department. All the Students who have passed B.A. or Shahadat-ul-Aliya from WafaqulMadaris and other Madaris are eligible for admission. Those candidates who have done only Shahadat-ul-Aliya are required to submit complete equivalence certificate issued by the University of Karachi.
Career opportunities
After passing M.A. there is vast scope in Schools, Colleges, Universities, Madarsas as teachers, as well as in other National and Multinational institutions as sarri’ah experts.
