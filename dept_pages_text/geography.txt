Chairman: Prof. Dr. Shamshad Akhtar.
Department of Geography is one of the pioneer departments of University of Karachi. It was established in 1952 under the chairmanship of Professor Dr. Manick B. Pithawala. Professor Oscar Schmider was one of the leading German Geographers who served as Chairman in the department. Department has been providing teaching and research in various fields of Geography for the last sixty years. Department has been served by leading Pakistani Geographers who were educated in USA, UK, Germany, India etc. The Department has an excellent academic history. The Department of Geography is the only Department among Universities of Pakistan which hosted International Geography Union (IGU) Conference. More than twenty Professors have served the Department as Chairpersons. Presently Prof. Dr. Sheeba Afsar is Chairperson. The Department has qualified academic staff which includes Professors, Assistant Professors and Visiting Faculty.
Career Opportunities
Geography graduates with Master's degree generally find useful employment in some major fields, i.e. education, administration, and private organizatio& other cellular companies, K-Electric, SSGC, Archeological Department, Planning Commission, and provincial departments of planning and development have employed geographers for their Geo-Spatial skills. Private firms, and Non-Governmental Organizations i.e. ECIL Consulting Services, Cabernet, Fiber optics, (NGOs) also utilize the expertise of geographers. There are various fields of application in Pakistan where geographer can explore job opportunities successfully to survive and Empowering Communities.
