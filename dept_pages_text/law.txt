Mr. Tauheedullah Siddiqui (In-charge)
Legal education is pivotal in building a just and organized society by preparing individuals to uphold the rule of law and contribute to social progress. In line with these ideals, the University of Karachi’s School of Law offers legal degree programs to provide high-quality legal training that addresses current demands in the field. With expanding opportunities in both domestic and international law sectors, our programs are meticulously designed to produce professionals skilled in legal strategy, problem-solving, and ethical decision-making.
Our Objectives
In-Depth Legal Training: Providing students with both theoretical insights and practical experience essential for effective legal practice.
Specialized Knowledge: Focusing on areas such as commercial law, public administration, and regulatory frameworks to prepare students for critical roles in diverse sectors.
National Development and Social Responsibility: Equipping graduates to make meaningful contributions to national growth and societal advancement through specialized legal expertise.
The BA-LLB (Hons) and LLM program at the University of Karachi’s School of Law aims to shape the future of legal professionals who are equipped to navigate the complexities of a globalized legal environment and drive positive change in society.
Career Prospects
As the legal profession evolves to meet global challenges, our graduates are well-equipped to excel in a variety of career paths. With an education that emphasizes adaptability, rigorous legal analysis, research proficiency, and a global perspective on diverse legal systems, they are prepared to navigate and thrive in a wide range of professional opportunities.
