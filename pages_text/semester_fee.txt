2. Admit Curd may not he issued to the defaulter by the Semester Cell.
3. Students of Morning program may not allow to sit in exam until the clearance of all outstanding dues.
4. Fee voucher may be downloaded at https://uok.edu.pk/semesterfees/ by using Kuick Pay (UBL) and pay using any of the following payment methods:
UBL Branches: The student or his/her agent can visit only branch with their downloaded fee voucher.
Kuick Pay: Student can pay semester fee by downloading Kuick pay App.
Al-Falah Branches: The student on his/her agent can visit campus branch only with their downloaded fee voucher.
5. For any query/help, please contact via Query from ( http://www.uok.edu.pk/semesterfees/query ). Email ( semesterfeee@uok.edu.pk ) by writing the Name. Seat No., Department. Degree Program, Semester(s), CNIC No., Cell Phone No., Email Address and the Query.
