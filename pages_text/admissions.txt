Instructions for Foreign Students:
All the students with a foreign nationality have to apply through their embassy and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Islamabad and they can apply till December 2023. Kindly read the admission policy carrying the detailed process for International Students given on the University web page under the office of the Foreign Students' Advisor, University of Karachi.
