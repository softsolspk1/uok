Faculty of Education
Dean: Prof. Dr. Samina Saeed (Officiating)
The Faculty of Education, was established in 1970 at the University of Karachi, and had been governing all colleges, teaching education in the city of Karachi.  Earlier, the Dean of the Education Faculty was selected from colleges on the basis of seniority. In 2003, for the first time, Dr. Fatima Razi, Professor, Department of Education was appointed as the Dean, Education.  Since then, the University realized that the Faculty of Education should be functional and finally the Faculty got approval in 2012 by the University statuary bodies and started working. The Faculty of Education comprised of the following three Departments became the part of the Faculty:
Department of Education
Department of Special Education
Department of Teacher Education
Besides, Departments, Faculty of Education also governs the functioning of all affiliated colleges, in the city of Karachi, teaching “Education” in general and Teacher Education in specific.
The Faculty is very active in developing linkages with other programs both at National and International levels. The target areas are teaching, research, publication and collaborative assignments between Universities and institutions both at National and International levels.

Faculty of Law
Dean: Barrister Muhammad Sarfaraz Ali Metlo
Barrister Metlo is a prominent lawyer (Advocate Supreme Court) and former President of the Sindh High Court Bar Association (SHCBA), Karachi. His appointment as Dean brings significant practical legal experience to the faculty, bridging academia and the professional bar. Many congratulations from the legal community highlight his reputation for leadership and expertise.
This role positions him as a key figure in shaping legal education in one of Pakistan’s oldest and largest public universities.
Key Aspects of the Role:
As Dean of the Faculty of Law (which includes the School of Law at UoK), responsibilities generally include:
Academic Leadership: Overseeing the overall academic direction of the Faculty/School of Law, including curriculum development, teaching standards, and ensuring compliance with Higher Education Commission (HEC) and Pakistan Bar Council requirements.
Administrative Head: Managing faculty affairs, including faculty appointments, performance, welfare, and coordination with departments and affiliated colleges.
Student Affairs: Supporting law students (LLB, LLM, PhD and related programs), addressing academic issues, promoting extracurricular activities (moot courts, seminars, internships), and fostering professional development to prepare students for legal practice.
Research and Development: Promoting legal research, organizing seminars, conferences, and collaborations with the legal community.
Representation: Acting as the official representative of the Faculty in university matters, interactions with the legal fraternity, courts, and external bodies. He also engages directly with students (e.g., motivational messages for exams and career building).
Honorary Nature: The position is held on a purely honorary basis, which is common for senior practicing lawyers in such roles.

Faculty
of Medicine
Dean: Professor. Dr. Fareeda Islam
The University of Karachi has 7 medical and dental colleges affiliated with it. The only public sector college is Karachi Medical & Dental College and the Dean, Faculty of Medicine is the senior most professor of Karachi Medical & Dental College. The colleges running both MBBS & BDS programmes are Karachi Medical & Dental College, Jinnah Medical Dental College and Liaquat College of Medicine and Dentistry. Those running BDS programs alone are Altamash Institute of Dental Medicine and Fatima Jinnah Dental College and those having MBBS programme alone are Liaquat National Medical College and Sir Syed College of Medicine. Admissions in this college are as per PMDC regulations.
Over the past years, there have been vast changes in the Undergraduate and Postgraduate programmes of the University in the fields of Medicine and Dentistry. The teaching methodology and the examination system of the University has changed from MCQ system to the internationally acclaimed Short Essay Questions and Best Choice Questions few years ago.  In this regard, training workshops for all faculty are held twice a year under the supervision of the Dean. The Board of Studies has constituted 2 committees, a Coordination Committee and a Quality Assurance Committee for examinations. The Semester system is being studied for possible implementation within the next year.
The Postgraduate programmes in clinical fields are the M.S. and M.D. programmes and in Basic Sciences, the MPhil, PhD. Details of these are available on the website .
The MS, MD programmes has changed from a 2 year programmes to a full 4 year, structured programme with 1 year course work at the University and 3 years clinical work in the supervisors department. For Admission, clearing the University of Karachi Entry Test is mandatory. Subsequently, candidates are chosen on the basis of merit. The  synopsis of each candidate is reviewed by the supervisor and the Dean before submission to the Board of Advanced Studies & Research, University of Karachi for final approval. Standardization of the Masters examination is ensured by having external examiners only, a change which has been implemented recently. The curriculum of each specialty is being reviewed by a M.S, M.D Curriculum Committee headed by the Dean.

Faculty
of Science
Dean: Prof. Dr. Bilquees Gul (Profile)
Welcome to the Faculty of Science at the University of Karachi one, of the largest and most comprehensive academic faculties of the University, with a long-standing tradition of excellence in teaching, research, and service. The Faculty plays a central role in advancing scientific education and knowledge in Pakistan.
The Faculty of Science comprises 21 academic departments spanning biological, physical, chemical, earth, environmental, mathematical, computational, agricultural, and health sciences. It offers a diverse range of undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral programs designed to provide students with strong theoretical foundations, rigorous practical training, and research-oriented learning. A centralized laboratory further supports interdisciplinary teaching and research through shared, state-of-the-art facilities.
Our academic programs namely BS, MSc/MS, MPhil, and PhD, are delivered by a highly qualified and dedicated faculty actively engaged in innovative research and scholarly activities. Through structured coursework, laboratory work, seminars, field training, and research projects, students are prepared to excel in academia, industry, healthcare, environmental management, biotechnology, and other scientific and technical fields.
In addition to its academic departments, the Faculty includes several nationally and internationally recognized institutes and centers of excellence, including the International Center for Chemical & Biological Sciences (ICCBS–H.E.J. Research Institute of Chemistry), Dr. A.Q. Khan Institute of Biotechnology and Genetic Engineering (KIBGE), Centre of Excellence in Marine Biology, Institute of Environmental Studies, Institute of Space Science & Technology, Institute of Marine Sciences, and the Dr. Muhammad Ajmal Khan Institute of Sustainable Halophyte Utilization (ISHU). These institutions significantly enrich the faculties research culture of the Faculty and provide advanced training opportunities for postgraduate students.
The Faculty of Science takes pride in its distinguished alumni who have made exceptional contributions to science and academia at national and international levels. Their achievements reflect the quality of education, rigorous training, and vibrant research environment fostered at the University of Karachi and continue to inspire future generations of scientists.
Research remains a cornerstone of the Faculty’s mission. Faculty members conduct cutting-edge, multidisciplinary research addressing contemporary scientific, societal, and environmental challenges. Supported by advanced laboratories and specialized research facilities, this work contributes meaningfully to national development and global scientific progress.
The Faculty also emphasizes industry engagement and community outreach through collaborative projects, internships, applied research initiatives, and outreach programs. These efforts promote innovation, knowledge transfer, and societal impact, ensuring that scientific education and research contribute effectively to economic growth and community well-being.
Committed to academic excellence, interdisciplinary collaboration, ethical research practices, and a student centered learning environment, the Faculty of Science continues to develop the skilled scientists, researchers, and professionals equipped to address current challenges and to contribute at national and international levels.
