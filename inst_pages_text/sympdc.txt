About SYMPDC
Importance of training in any field of life can hardly be over-emphasized. Functional and operational efficiency of an organization, establishment or institution predominantly depends on the quality of training of its members/staff. The higher the standard of training, greater the efficiency. With this basic premise in mind, Sardar Yasin Malik Professional Development Centre (SYMPDC) was established on 29th October 2010. The aim of the
Centre is to organize job-oriented and focused training for Entrepreneurs, Executives, Academia, Business/ Admin
Professionals and university level Students with the object of enhancing their knowledge and expertise to
enable them to face the challenges of their career with efficiency and confidence. Sardar Muhammad Yasin
Malik, Hilal-e-Imtiaz, Sitara-e-Imtiaz, an eminent industrialist, a former Federal and Provincial Minister is the
founder and the moving spirit behind this fledgling institution. He has not only provided financial support for
establishment and smooth miming of the Centre, but also takes keen interest in its activities and development as an efficient training set-up. SYMPDC is functioning with a professional zeal to establish itself as an accomplished training facility.
