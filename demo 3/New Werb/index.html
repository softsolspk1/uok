About the University

Established by an act of parliament, seventy-five years ago.

The Karachi University Act was passed on 23 October 1950 and enacted the following year, with Prof. A. B. A. Haleem as the university’s first Vice Chancellor. Teaching began in 1952 with two faculties and fifty students — after two years spent solely examining the affiliated colleges of Sindh. On 18 January 1960 the campus moved to its 1,279-acre site on University Road, a date still marked jointly by alumni and the administration each year. In 2026 the university celebrates its Platinum Jubilee: seventy-five years of teaching, research, and public service from the same address.
