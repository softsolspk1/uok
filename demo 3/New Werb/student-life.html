Campus Life

1,279 acres, at the centre of the city.

One of the largest single-campus universities in the country, on University Road since 1960 — with the Dr. Mahmud Husain Library, academic blocks, and research institutes sharing the same grounds.

On Campus

A self-contained academic city.

350,000+ books

Alumni Network

Wherever you are, you’re still Karachi University.

The University of Karachi Alumni (UKA) represents graduates across Social and Biological Sciences, Management and Administrative Sciences, Pharmacy, Islamic Studies, Law, Medicine, Engineering, and Education — a reservoir of talent and expertise spanning the world.

An office within the Public Relations Office handles membership administration and works to build a cohesive alumni network, with timely announcements of university events throughout the year.

Stay Connected

Keeping alumni informed about fellow graduates’ achievements and university developments.

Build the Network

Organizing alumni-based activities that connect graduates around the globe.

Give Back

Growing an endowment fund supporting current and future students.
