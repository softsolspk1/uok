Research

Twenty institutes, one research culture.

Beyond the 53 teaching departments, the university runs twenty dedicated research institutes and centres — from world-recognised chemistry to marine biology on the Arabian Sea.

20Institutes & centres
Flagship

Where Pakistani chemistry earned its global reputation.

H.E.J. Research Institute of Chemistry

The university’s prime research institute, internationally recognised for natural product chemistry and designated by the World Academy of Sciences. Part of the International Center for Chemical & Biological Sciences (ICCBS).

Dr. Panjwani Center for Molecular Medicine & Drug Research

Part of the International Center for Chemical & Biological Sciences (ICCBS), driving drug discovery and molecular medicine research.
