Governance Bodies

Syndicate & former Vice Chancellors.

The Syndicate is the university’s principal executive and governing body; its full membership and a record of former Vice Chancellors are maintained by the Registrar’s office.

Directorates & Offices

Fifteen directorates and offices support admissions, examinations, student affairs, finance, and campus operations — each reporting through the Registrar’s office.
