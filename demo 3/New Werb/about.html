Est. 1950 · Platinum Jubilee 2026

Seventy-five years on University Road.

The University of Karachi was established by an act of parliament and has grown into Pakistan’s largest public research university — eight faculties, fifty-three departments, and twenty research institutes and centres.

Our Story

From an act of parliament to a 1,279-acre campus.

The Karachi University Act was passed on 23 October 1950 and enacted the following year, with Prof. A. B. A. Haleem serving as the university’s first Vice Chancellor. For its first two years the university existed solely to examine the affiliated colleges of Sindh; direct teaching did not begin until 1952, with two faculties and fifty students. In 2026 the university marks its Platinum Jubilee — seventy-five years of teaching, research, and public service.
