History

One of Karachi’s first computer science departments.

The Department of Computer Science was established by a resolution of the Academic Council on 27 November 1984, and began functioning in the 1985–86 academic year — offering a Master of Computer Science (MCS) degree and becoming one of the first institutions in Karachi to teach Computer Science and Technology.

The department maintains close links with professional organizations at national and international levels, and revises its course content every two years to keep pace with developments in the field — curricula are built in line with the recommendations of the Sindh Task Force on Computer Science Programs.
